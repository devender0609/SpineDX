# SpineDx-Tx v15 Implementation Map

This build operationalizes the 23-point improvement plan as a research framework. It does not claim clinical validation.

1. Narrow intended-use scope and explicit out-of-scope detector.
2. Separate safety, syndrome, localization, treatment eligibility, fusion, and future outcome questions.
3. Level-by-level L1-S1 imaging matrix.
4. Explicit exiting/traversing root mapping and ranked candidate targets.
5. Bilateral motor, sensory, reflex, functional testing, quality and confidence.
6. Graded neurologic deficit hierarchy.
7. Granular cauda equina screen including PVR and urinary symptom distinctions.
8. Claudication provocation/relief, bicycle/uphill pattern, and competing vascular findings.
9. Decompression-first logic and independent fusion-rationale requirement.
10. Patient goals, preference, risk tolerance, work demand, and support.
11. ODI, PROMIS PF, PROMIS Pain Interference, depression, and ZCQ placeholders.
12. Surgical risk and optimization separated from appropriateness.
13. Synthetic percentages removed from the primary clinical display.
14. Explicit applicability and uncertainty output.
15. Out-of-scope detection for major exclusions.
16. Clinician adjudication workflow supported through export.
17. De-identified consecutive-patient research export.
18. Baseline outcome fields structured for longitudinal follow-up.
19. Validation protocol retains calibration and decision-curve requirements for future real models.
20. Export schema supports subgroup/fairness analysis.
21. Clinician agreement, override reason, and final decision logging.
22. Rule-level evidence traceability.
23. Inputs, contradictions, limitations, evidence, and model version remain reviewable by clinicians.
