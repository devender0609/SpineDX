# Prespecified outcome definitions for future validation

The current app does not predict these outcomes. Future real-data models must define each endpoint and horizon before analysis.

- Emergency escalation: same-day emergency evaluation recommended by the adjudication panel.
- Exact localization: complete agreement on syndrome, side, root, lumbar level, and compression zone.
- Decompression consultation: expert-adjudicated appropriateness of surgical consultation, not surgery itself.
- Independent fusion rationale: at least one prespecified instability, deformity, collapse, revision-instability, pseudarthrosis, or destabilizing-resection rationale.
- Major medical complication: prespecified 30-day composite.
- Surgical-site infection: superficial and deep outcomes reported separately at 30 and 90 days.
- Non-home discharge: skilled nursing or inpatient rehabilitation after the index admission.
- Persistent opioid use: prespecified continuous or recurrent opioid use at 90 days, 6 months, and 12 months.
- Pseudarthrosis: fusion-specific endpoint with prespecified imaging criteria and assessment time.
