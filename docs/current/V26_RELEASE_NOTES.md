# v26 clinical-validation release

- Explicit typed blank and demonstration factories.
- Four-state representation for clinical yes/no findings.
- Measurement objects distinguish measured, not measured, unknown, and not applicable.
- Independent syndrome derivation separated from clinician-entered phenotype.
- Initial localization scope narrowed to L4, L5, S1 and multiroot central stenosis.
- Clinical eligibility required before imaging targets are ranked.
- Reflex, motor, sensory, tension-sign, mimic, and missingness evidence shown separately.
- Indeterminate urgency when required safety findings are incomplete.
- Level-specific fusion assessment and unable-to-assess state.
- Patient-specific, procedure-specific, and general educational risk information separated.
- Symmetric first-reviewer, second-reviewer, and adjudicated reference-standard records.
- Synthetic emulator removed from the validation build.
- Color-coded summary cards use red only for emergency findings, amber for urgent caution, green for documented reassuring/supportive findings, blue for informational support, and gray for uncertainty.
