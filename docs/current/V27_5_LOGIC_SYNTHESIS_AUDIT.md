# v27.5 Logic-first synthesis audit

This release hardens cross-section consistency and makes the synthesis case-specific.

- Progressive-weakness contradictions block synthesis and prevent a routine urgency label.
- Proposed procedure levels must have corresponding imaging documentation.
- Injection level and side are reconciled with imaging and symptom laterality.
- Missing-information output is generated from actual contradictions rather than a generic list.
- The synthesis includes a domain-level concordance map.
- Evidence shown in the synthesis is dynamic: only triggered rules are included. The Evidence tab remains the broader static library.
- Fusion evidence and fusion cards are omitted when fusion is not being considered.
- The lumbar level matrix drives level-completeness automatically.
- Visual hierarchy prioritizes urgency, concordance, unresolved conflicts, and next steps.
