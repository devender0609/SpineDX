# Intended use — v26
SpineDx-Tx v26 is a transparent clinical decision-support and research data-capture prototype for adults with suspected L4, L5, or S1 radiculopathy caused by lumbar disc herniation, lateral-recess stenosis, or foraminal stenosis, and adults with suspected neurogenic claudication caused by central lumbar stenosis.

The application does not independently diagnose disease, select surgery, predict treatment benefit, or calculate individualized complication probabilities. Clinicians must independently review the patient, examination, and imaging.

Treatment-reconciliation outputs are outside scope for pediatric cases, pregnancy, cervical/thoracic disease, tumor, infection, acute fracture, major deformity, prior long fusion, complex neuromuscular disease, or predominantly axial pain. Safety screening remains available.
